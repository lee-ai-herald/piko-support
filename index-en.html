Piko Support

Piko is a macOS workspace with a Reminders board, Pomodoro timer, clipboard history, and notes. It requires macOS 13 or later.

Contact Us

Open an issue on GitHub and we will reply there: https://github.com/lee-ai-herald/piko-support/issues

Issues are public. Do not include passwords, private tasks, or clipboard content.

The board says it isn't connected to Reminders

Click Continue on the board and allow access. If you declined before, turn on Piko in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Reminders. Other features don't need this permission.

Nothing is recorded in the clipboard

Clipboard history is off by default. Turn it on in the Clipboard tab or in Settings > Tools. Content marked confidential by password managers is not recorded.

No Pomodoro notifications

Allow notifications for Piko in System Settings > Notifications, and make sure Notify When Finished is on in Settings > Tools.

I can't find the Piko window

Click the Piko icon in the Dock or menu bar. You can show or hide the Dock icon in Settings > General.

Shortcuts don't work

They may conflict with another app. Change them in Settings > Shortcuts.

Trial and purchase

The App Store version includes a free 7-day trial with no automatic charge. A one-time purchase unlocks Piko forever. On a new Mac or after reinstalling, click Restore Purchases in Settings > Data & About > Purchase.

How to use a free code

On macOS 15 or later, open Settings > Data & About > Purchase > Redeem Code and enter the code. You can also open the redemption link you received. On earlier versions, use the link, or click your picture in the App Store and choose Redeem Gift Card or Code. Then return to Piko. If it isn't unlocked automatically, click Restore Purchases.
