Piko Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Piko does not collect, upload, or sell your personal data. It contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking code. The App Store version has no network access permission. The direct version connects to GitHub only to check for and download updates, and GitHub's privacy policy applies to those requests.

Reminders

The board reads, creates, changes, and deletes your Apple Reminders only after you allow access. The data is managed by the Reminders app. If you use iCloud, Apple handles syncing. Pomodoro, clipboard history, and notes work without this permission.

Clipboard History

Off by default. When you turn it on, Piko reads the text and images you copy in the background, along with the name of the app you copied from, and saves them only on this Mac without encryption. Content marked as confidential, temporary, or automatically generated, such as by password managers, is not recorded. Items that are not favorites are deleted automatically after the limit you set.

Notes and Settings

Notes, schedules, and settings are saved only on this Mac.

Purchases

Trials, purchases, restores, and offer codes are handled by Apple's App Store. Piko only reads your purchase status on this Mac. It never sees your payment information and does not send purchase records to the developer.

Notifications

Piko asks for notification permission when you first start the Pomodoro timer. Notifications are only used on this Mac to tell you that a session has ended. Remote push is not used.

Retention and Deletion

You can delete tasks, notes, and clipboard items in Piko at any time, turn off and clear clipboard history in Settings > Tools, and revoke Reminders and notification access in System Settings. Deleting the app and its data container removes all local data. Tasks already saved to Apple Reminders must be deleted in Reminders.

External Links

Links in About open external websites in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Changes and Contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a date. For questions, contact the developer through the support page: https://lee-ai-herald.github.io/piko-support/index-en.html
